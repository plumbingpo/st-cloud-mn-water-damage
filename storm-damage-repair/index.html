Wind-driven rain pushes water in through damaged roofing, broken windows and loose siding. A roof leak can soak insulation, ceilings and the cavities inside your walls. Lower levels flood when storm drains are overwhelmed by a sudden surge of water. Maintenance Tips for Storm Damage Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Extend downspouts and slope the soil so runoff moves away from the foundation
  • Clean out downspouts and gutters regularly, which lets heavy rainfall drain away as it should
  • Have your roof checked annually and fix vulnerable areas ahead of the stormy months
  • In areas that see hurricanes, fit storm shutters or windows rated for impact
  • Branches break in high wind, so keep nearby trees pruned away from the roof and siding
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Downspouts knocked off and gutter runs hanging after high wind
  • Wet insulation in the attic or wall cavities found after bad weather
  • Flashing that is bent, loose or missing at roof edges, valleys and vents
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground
  • A musty smell indoors in the days after heavy rain
  • Ceiling drywall that sags or bulges, or paint that bubbles after rain
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
