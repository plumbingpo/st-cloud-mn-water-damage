How to Tell You Need Water Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Door casings and baseboards have warped or pulled off the wall surface.
  • Since the water damage, certain doors drag on the jamb or miss the latch.
  • Where water ran down a wall, the paint has blistered, peeled or turned brown.
  • At floor level, cabinet panels and bases show swelling, peeling or soft spots.
  • The edges of your laminate or vinyl planks have puffed up, curled or pulled apart.
  • During drying, sections of wallboard came out and the framing is still exposed.
  • Hardwood boards have cupped, crowned or lifted and did not flatten after drying.

Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.
  • Future repairs match better when you have held on to extra flooring, tile and the paint information.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • When kitchens, bathrooms or laundry rooms are rebuilt, choose backer board and moisture resistant drywall.
