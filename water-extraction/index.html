Water extraction is the most critical first step in any water damage restoration project. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. St. Cloud Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout St. Cloud using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. Confined spaces, wall cavities and the gaps between floor layers call for different gear, so we use portable extractors, specialty tools and submersible pumps in places the truck-mounted hoses cannot get to.

Professional water extraction is not simply about removing the standing water you can see. Water migrates behind baseboards, below flooring, into wall cavities and under cabinets, and our technicians, who are IICRC-certified, locate it with moisture detection tools. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

Signs It Is Time for Water Extraction

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A basement taking on water after heavy rain or a sump pump failure
  • Wet baseboards and water creeping from beneath kitchen or bath cabinets
  • Ongoing leakage from the plumbing, the air conditioning or an appliance
  • Floors flooded by a fire sprinkler or suppression system going off
  • Pooled water on the carpet, or a soggy feel with every step
  • Water entering through a flooded basement stairwell or window well
  • Standing water on any floor surface, whatever the source

Water Extraction Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Look beneath sinks, behind toilets and beside appliances every so often to catch leaks early
  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • Program (860) 318-2305 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Keep a basic emergency water kit: old towels, a bucket and your restoration company's number
  • Install automatic water shutoff valves that detect leaks and stop the flow
  • Pour water into the sump pit a few times a year to confirm the pump kicks on

The Benefits of Professional Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. A shop vacuum might need a whole day for what our truck-mounted equipment finishes in minutes, because professional extraction outpaces consumer gear hundreds of times over. That gap in speed is more than a convenience, because each hour water touches building materials can add thousands of dollars in further damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. We go after those hidden deposits with specialty tools and methods, and that takes out far more water than extracting the surface only. This thorough approach reduces drying time, lowers equipment costs and, most importantly, prevents the hidden mold growth that results from trapped moisture.
